Pre-Award
1
Upload or Paste Your RFQ
DIVISION 2 — CONTRACT REGISTRY
Provide the solicitation in any format. The system extracts everything it needs automatically.
You can provide an RFQ by:
  • Pasting the SAM.gov link
  • Uploading the PDF
  • Pasting the text
  • Dropping in the CLIN table
The system automatically extracts:
  • CLINs
  • Quantities
  • Delivery info
  • NAICS / PSC
  • Set-aside
  • Compliance language
  • Deadlines
  • Attachments required
2
Review the Auto-Generated Draft
DIVISION 3 — BID PIPELINE
Once the contract is registered, the system builds your complete bid package automatically.
The system builds:
  • CLIN pricing table
  • Capability statement
  • Compliance packet
  • Submission packet (internal only)
You review everything before submitting. Nothing is sent externally.
3
Submit Your Bid Manually
DIVISION 3 — SUBMISSION
Download the packet and submit through your chosen channel. The system never submits on your behalf.
Submit through:
  • SAM.gov
  • Email to the CO
  • Agency portal
You stay in full control of all external submissions.
4
When Awarded, Activate the Pipeline
DIVISION 3 — AWARD
Mark the bid as AWARDED to unlock the post-award workflow.
DRAFT → SUBMITTED → AWARDED
Post-Award
5
Create Your Purchase Order
DIVISION 4 — PURCHASE ORDERS
Click Create PO.
The system auto-creates:
  • PO number
  • Vendor
  • Delivery info
  • Line items
DRAFT → SENT → ACKNOWLEDGED → FULFILLED
6
Create Your Shipment
DIVISION 5 — LOGISTICS & FULFILLMENT
Click Create Shipment.
The system auto-creates:
  • Shipment record
  • Carrier
  • Tracking number
PENDING → IN_TRANSIT → DELIVERED
7
Create Your Invoice
DIVISION 9 — FINANCIALS
Click Create Invoice.
The system auto-creates:
  • Invoice number
  • Due date
  • Line items
DRAFT → SENT → PARTIAL → PAID
8
Record Payment
DIVISION 9 — PAYMENT RECONCILIATION
When the CO pays, click Record Payment.
The system updates:
  • Invoice status
  • Financial summary
  • Outstanding balance
