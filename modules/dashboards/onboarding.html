POST-AWARD
From Award to Payment Received
05
DIV 4
Create Your Purchase Order
DIVISION 4 — PURCHASE ORDERS
One command auto-creates the purchase order from the awarded bid. Vendor, line items, and delivery info are all pre-filled.
⚡ Create PO POST /division/4/purchase-orders/from-bid/{bidId}
PO number (PO-001…)
Vendor pre-filled
Delivery info
All line items copied
DRAFT → SENT → ACKNOWLEDGED → FULFILLED
06
DIV 5
Create Your Shipment
DIVISION 5 — LOGISTICS & FULFILLMENT
One command creates the shipment record from the PO. Update the carrier and tracking number when goods ship.
📦 Create Shipment POST /division/5/shipments/from-po/{poId}
Shipment number (SHIP-001…)
Vendor linked
Carrier + tracking fields
Expected delivery date
PENDING → IN_TRANSIT → DELIVERED
07
DIV 9
Create Your Invoice
DIVISION 9 — FINANCIALS & BILLING
One command generates the invoice from the awarded bid. All line items and pricing are pre-populated. Set your due date.
🧾 Create Invoice POST /division/9/invoices/from-bid/{bidId}
Invoice number (INV-001…)
Due date set
Line items pre-filled
Agency name pre-filled
DRAFT → SENT → PARTIAL → PAID
08
DIV 9
Record Payment
DIVISION 9 — PAYMENT RECONCILIATION
When the contracting officer pays, record the amount. The system updates the invoice status, financial summary, and outstanding balance automatically.
✓ Record Payment POST /division/9/invoices/{id}/payment
Invoice status → PAID
Financial summary updated
Outstanding balance cleared
Payment timestamp logged
Check your full financial position at any time: GET /division/9/summary — total billed, collected, and outstanding across all contracts.

HOW TO USE IT
1
Feed the system an RFQ
Paste the link, upload the PDF, or paste the text. Any format works.
2
Review the draft
The system builds everything automatically. You review pricing, line items, and the capability statement before anything is submitted.
3
Submit manually
Download the packet. Upload to SAM.gov, email the CO, or use the agency portal. You stay in control of all external submissions.
4
When awarded, click Create PO
Division 4 handles the purchase order. Vendor, line items, and status chain — all automatic.
5
Click Create Shipment
Division 5 handles fulfillment. Update the carrier and tracking number when goods ship.
6
Click Create Invoice
Division 9 handles billing. Line items and pricing are pre-populated. Set your due date and send.
7
Record payment
When the CO pays, record the amount. Financials update automatically — outstanding balance, payment timestamp, and status all update in real time.
WHAT THE SYSTEM DOES NOT DO
  • Submit bids externally — all external submissions are done by you
  • Email contracting officers — you initiate all agency communication
  • Commit your company to anything — you review and approve every step
You stay in control. The system does the work.
SUPPORT
Your Operator Engine logs every action in every division. A complete audit trail is maintained automatically. If you need help, your key identifies your session — every request is logged by division, operation, and timestamp.
