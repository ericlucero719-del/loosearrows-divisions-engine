# Division 10 — Operator Bot (Onboarding & Guidance)

The Operator Bot is your automated guide inside the LooseArrows Divisions Engine. 
It explains each step, routes tasks to the correct division, and keeps your contracting 
pipeline moving from RFQ intake to payment received.

---

## What the Operator Bot Does

- Guides you through the RFQ → Bid → Award → PO → Shipment → Invoice → Payment pipeline  
- Explains what each division does and when to use it  
- Monitors statuses across all divisions  
- Alerts you when an action is required  
- Provides step-by-step instructions for every stage  
- Ensures nothing stalls or gets stuck  
- Maintains chain-of-custody and activity logs  

---

## How to Use the Operator Bot

### 1. Start with an RFQ
Type or click:
**"I have an RFQ"**

The bot will:
- Ask how you want to upload it  
- Route it to Division 2  
- Confirm extraction and pricing  
- Notify you when the draft is ready  

---

### 2. Review Your Bid
Type:
**"Review my bid"**

The bot will:
- Open your draft  
- Show CLINs, pricing, and compliance packet  
- Explain what to check before submission  

---

### 3. Mark as Submitted
Type:
**"Mark submitted"**

The bot will:
- Update status  
- Log the submission  
- Move the contract into the waiting stage  

---

### 4. When Awarded
Type:
**"We got awarded"**

The bot will:
- Route to Division 4  
- Create your Purchase Order  
- Explain next steps  

---

### 5. Create Your PO
Type:
**"Create PO"**

The bot will:
- Auto-generate the PO  
- Show status timeline  
- Prepare for shipment creation  

---

### 6. Create Your Shipment
Type:
**"Create shipment"**

The bot will:
- Auto-generate the shipment  
- Ask for carrier + tracking  
- Track delivery status  

---

### 7. Create Your Invoice
Type:
**"Create invoice"**

The bot will:
- Auto-generate the invoice  
- Set due date  
- Track payment status  

---

### 8. Record Payment
Type:
**"Payment received"**

The bot will:
- Update invoice  
- Update financial summary  
- Close out the contract  

---

## Operator Bot Commands (Quick Reference)

- **"I have an RFQ"** — Start a new contract  
- **"Review my bid"** — Open your draft  
- **"Mark submitted"** — Move to waiting stage  
- **"We got awarded"** — Trigger post-award pipeline  
- **"Create PO"** — Generate purchase order  
- **"Create shipment"** — Generate shipment  
- **"Create invoice"** — Generate invoice  
- **"Payment received"** — Close out the contract
