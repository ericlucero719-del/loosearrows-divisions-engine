## RFQ Intake (Division 2)

Upload or paste:
- SAM.gov link
- PDF file
- Raw text
- CLIN table

System automatically extracts:
- CLINs + quantities
- Delivery info
- NAICS / PSC
- Set-aside
- Compliance language
- Deadlines
- Attachments required

Outputs generated:
- CLIN pricing table
- Capability statement
- Compliance packet
- Submission packet (internal)
